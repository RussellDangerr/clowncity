# Clown City v0.2.1 — Smoother Rolling

A feel-and-polish patch on top of the v0.2.0 unicycle rework: fix the momentum
snag on flat ground, and give Poko an actual unicycle that leans with his momentum.
No new mechanics; no gameplay balance changes.

## Fixed

### Poko snagged on flat ground, killing momentum
`resolveCollisions()` resolves the X axis before the Y axis and decided "this tile
is a wall" with `if (overlapX < overlapY)`. While grounded and rolling, gravity
sinks the hitbox a few px into the floor each frame (worse right after a landing,
and proportionally worse on treadmills where the per-frame step is smaller). As the
leading edge crossed a flat tile seam, there were frames where the tiny horizontal
entry overlap was smaller than that vertical penetration — so the **next flat-floor
tile was misclassified as a wall and `vx` was zeroed**. Tile iteration order is
undefined, so the "next" tile could win the frame. The result was an intermittent
catch that bled speed.

Fix: a **step-tolerance guard** — the X-pass (and the moving-platform side) now only
block horizontally when the tile rises into the body by more than `stepTolerance`
(8px > the 6px max per-frame fall penetration, < a real step). Flat seams are
ignored; real walls (and the full-height level edges, `overlapY = 28`) still hard-stop.

Verified deterministically: across **187 flat tile-seam crossings, `vx` held at 300
with zero dips**; treadmills hold a clean 150; a run into a boundary wall still stops
dead.

## New

### Poko rides a real unicycle (procedural)
The player was a flat rectangle. It's now a procedural unicycle drawn in
`_drawRectFallback()`: a **spinning spoked wheel** at the ground-contact point, a
short frame, and Poko's body + eyes + a small red clown nose perched just above.
The whole rig **leans with momentum** — a gentle tilt into the direction of travel
that **rocks back during a reversal** as the wheel kicks out the old way (the
braking lunge). It's deliberately subtle and grounded. Purely cosmetic — the hitbox
and all collision/combat are unchanged.

New tunables in `player.js`: `stepTolerance` (8), `cruiseLean` (0.10), `brakeLean`
(0.14), `leanRate` (10), `wheelRadius` (8). New design tokens: `wheel`, `frame`,
`clownNose`.

## How to play
Check out `v0.2.1` and open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.

**Controls:** swipe (or ← →) to steer, tap (or Space) to jump. Reverse into a
monster to whack it, or stomp it from above. Don't fall in the gaps.
